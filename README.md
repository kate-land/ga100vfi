# GA100VFI

This repository used to contain materials relevant to my proof-of-concept of the
[GA100 voltage fault injection unlock](https://blog.kate.land/posts/using-voltage-glitching-to-unlock-the-a100/). At the time of writing, others have
devised much more user-friendly and effective implementations of GA100VFI. As
such, I have elected to provide links to a few of these repositories instead of
keeping my obsolete PCB design and firmware in place, as its presence is more
misleading than helpful to newcomers.

Hardware hacking is always performed at your own risk! I am not responsible for
any success or failure that arises from your usage of these repos or my writeup.

Good luck!
